About Our Church

Rooted in faith, grounded in history, called to serve.

Our Story

A Legacy of Faith & Service

A.M.E. ST. Joseph's Church is a proud congregation of the African Methodist Episcopal Church, one of the oldest and most historic Black denominations in the United States. Founded on the principles of freedom, justice, and the love of God, we are committed to nurturing spiritual growth, serving our community, and uplifting our members through faith, education, and action.

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To minister to the spiritual, intellectual, physical, emotional, and environmental needs of all people by spreading Christ's liberating gospel through word and deed.

👁️

Our Vision

To be a Christ-centered community of believers who transform lives through worship, fellowship, discipleship, ministry, and evangelism.

📜

Our Beliefs

We believe in the Holy Trinity — Father, Son, and Holy Spirit — and the transforming power of the Gospel of Jesus Christ as the foundation of our faith and practice.
